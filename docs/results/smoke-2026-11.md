# Smoke run with local models, November 2026

This is a pilot, not a result. There are 8 pairs and one judge, so a 1-point difference is within noise. Its purpose is to find faults and to plan the full study (see [paper/outline.md](../paper/outline.md)).

## Setup

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Pairs | the eight of `examples/demo/pairs_smoke.jsonl`: 6 subjects (company, sole proprietor, self-employed, household, UK company, farm); 5 in Russian, 3 in English; one with a prompt injection |
| Author, reviewer | qwen3.5:9b, gemma4:12b (Ollama, Q4, 16k context) |
| Judge | ministral-3:14b, a third family; scores 6 criteria from 0 to 2, total /12 |
| Systems | `full`: the agent. `single_call`: one call with the same context. `retelling`: a summary of the news |
| Code version | `refract eval run` before the fixes below; the answers are in `examples/demo/recordings/qwen9b_gemma12b.json` |

`full` against the baselines, on the judge's total:

- against `retelling`: 6 wins, 1 tie, 1 loss; mean difference +1.50 [0.12–2.75]; sign test p = 0.125;

Per run, `full` made 4.5 model calls and 0.75 revisions. All 8 runs gave an insight.

## What the numbers say

1. **Grounding versus judged quality.** The agent writes clearly better than a retelling. One judge does not see it as better than a single call with the same context. What separates the two is what code finds: a single call broke a rule in half of its answers and averaged 0.25 invented numbers per answer; the agent had neither. The judge does not penalise those faults. The full study needs a human or rule-aware judgement next to the LLM judge (RQ1, RQ6).
2. **Impact calibration.** The judge found the agent's impact level right in only 38% of answers, against 62% for a single call. In one answer the agent said the news did not concern the subject (a self-employed designer and the VAT threshold of the simplified system), yet rated the impact medium negative. The impact ceiling allowed it: the subject's sensitivity to "taxes" matched any tax news.
3. **Russian answers score lower** for every system: 5.4 for `full` in Russian against 7.7 in English, and the same gap for the baselines. Because the gap is the same everywhere, it is more likely a trait of the judge or of the Russian demo pairs than of the agent. Check it with a second judge and with human scores (RQ5).
4. **Time.** At 181 s per pair against 37 s for a single call, the agent costs about five times the time. The plan, review and revision account for the difference.

## Faults found in the answers, and what changed

| Fault | Example | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A tax news item about one regime linked strongly to a subject on another | VAT threshold of the simplified system (УСН) → the self-employed designer (НПД): impact medium | `agent/impact.py`: tax news that names a regime is a weak link for a subject on another regime, so the ceiling for the designer is `low` while the coffee shop on УСН keeps `high` |
| An internal counterparty code shown to the reader | "Since you import from China (counterparty cp_import_309)" | `checks.name_counterparties`: a code is replaced by what is known, e.g. "the supplier with 14% of outgoing payments", noted in the trace |
| A derived number computed wrongly | "every 10% of freight cuts the margin by 1.5 pp" → "a 15% jump cuts it by about 1.5 pp" (should be about 2.25) | rule check `arithmetic` (`checks/arithmetic.py`): proportional rules are read from the sources and every plain application of one is recomputed; a wrong one is an error naming the right value |
| An unrelated house view attached | the VAT threshold view in an answer about competitors closing coffee shops | rule check `off_topic`: a house view citing only theses whose factors are not the event's is an error and is cut (a sector tag alone does not count: the VAT thesis is tagged “catering”) |
| Generic fallback action | "Обсудить влияние события с менеджером" when the model's actions were cut | by design. The policy keeps a safe step instead of none; counted by the fallback note in the trace |

## For the full study

- Use more pairs: all subject × event × role triples, both languages, and the parity set.
- Use two judges from different families, plus human scores of the same answers (`refract eval label`).
- Report rule errors and invented numbers next to the judge's score, as primary outcomes rather than footnotes.
- Run the impact study (RQ3) on human labels: `refract eval relevance` and the `gold_impact` column of the pairs.
- Rerun after the two changes above, to see whether the judge's calibration score for `full` rises.

## Second run, after the fixes

A clean folder (`runs/smoke3`), the same models (qwen3.5:9b author, gemma4:12b reviewer), two judges: ministral-3:14b and phi4:14b. The baselines' answers are the same as in the first run.

| System | ministral total /12 | phi4 total /12 | Impact calibrated (ministral) | Rule errors (code) | Invented numbers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `full` | 5.75 | 9.38 | 50% (38% in the first run) | 0% | 0.00 |
| `single_call` | 7.00 | 11.12 | 62% | 62% | 0.25 |
| `retelling` | 4.75 | 10.25 | 50% | — | — |

- **The judges barely agree.** Spearman on totals is 0.24 and κ on the overall score 0.15; per criterion κ runs from −0.07 to 0.24. They agree on the order of `full` and `single_call`, but phi4 also puts `retelling` above `full`. At this scale an LLM judge's ranking is not evidence. Human scores of the same answers are needed before any claim about quality.
- **Impact calibration improved** for the agent, from 38% to 50%, after the tax-regime link and the code-side changes. This is n = 8.
- **Actions are the agent's weakest criterion for both judges** (0.62 and 1.38 against 1.00 and 2.00 for a single call). The traces show why. The checks cut actions that are unsupported, that name a product no fired rule offers, or that are wrong for the kind of subject. That left one action in five of eight answers, often the generic "discuss with the manager". A single call keeps two or three actions, because nothing checks them. Change: fewer than two actions are now topped up from fired policy rules (see CHANGELOG).
- **The trade-off for the paper:** code checks remove what is wrong, but they also leave less behind. The judges reward the fuller answer even when a rule check finds errors in 62% of them. The claim to test with people: is a shorter answer without errors preferred to a fuller one with them?

## A third judge

The same 24 answers of the second run were scored by Claude Opus 5.5, by the same rubric, from the same context and answer (`judge/claude-opus-5-5/`, each with a comment and the unsupported statements). It is a third automatic judge, not a person.

| System | Total /12 | Overall /5 | Actions /2 | House view /2 | Grounding /2 | Impact calibrated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `full` | 9.0 | 3.1 | 1.25 | 2.00 | 1.50 | 88% |
| `single_call` | 9.9 | 3.8 | 1.75 | 1.88 | 1.25 | 75% |
| `retelling` | 6.5 | 2.4 | 1.62 | 0.25 | 0.00 | 75% |

- All three judges rank `single_call` above `full`; this judge agrees that the gap is in actions (one action in five answers of eight) and finds the agent best on house view, grounding and impact.
- The retelling made up a "bank position" in seven answers of eight; the single call reversed a causal link twice ("a stronger dollar gives fewer pounds", "the largest customer delayed purchases" — the news says foreign buyers did).
- Two faults of the agent this reading found are now caught by code: a rule applied without scaling ("~1.5 pp" for a 15% freight jump), and a headline that still states a statement cut from the text.
- Agreement with the two local judges is better than theirs with each other, but still poor: Spearman 0.49 and 0.41 on totals, κ 0.27 and 0.12 on the overall score.

## Limitations of these results

- **Too few pairs.** Eight pairs from six invented subjects. A difference of one point out of 12 is within the confidence intervals.
- **No human labels.** Three LLM judges that agree poorly are not evidence of quality. The claim that the agent's answers are better for a reader stays untested.
- **Synthetic data.** The subjects, the news and the "Demo Bank" research were written for the demo. Real profiles and real news are messier.
- **One model pair.** The agent's design was tuned on the same small local models it was tested with.
- **Not blind.** The author of the fixes also chose the pairs and read the answers.
