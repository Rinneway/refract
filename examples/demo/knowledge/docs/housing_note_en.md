# Demo Bank Research — Housing market note

This document is fictional and was written to demonstrate Refract.

## Demand

We expect new-build sales to fall by 15–20% in 2026 as market mortgage rates stay above 20%. Demand will recover only after the policy rate falls below 12%.

## Developers and suppliers

Developers are likely to stretch payments to suppliers and contractors. We see the main risk for building-materials wholesalers in longer receivables rather than in lower volumes.
