# Demo Bank Research — FX and commodities monthly

This document is fictional and was written to demonstrate Refract. Figures and forecasts do not describe the real economy.

## US dollar

We expect the dollar to stay strong into year-end because US inflation is proving sticky. Our forecast for GBP/USD at the end of 2026 is 1.24, down from 1.29 a month ago.

For UK exporters billing in dollars, a stronger dollar raises sterling revenue; we recommend hedging at least half of the expected dollar receipts for the next two quarters.

## Grain

We expect Black Sea milling wheat prices to stay in the range of USD 235–255 a tonne until the end of the year. Freight costs are the main risk: every 10% rise in freight cuts the exporter's margin by about 1.5 percentage points.

## Oil

We keep our Brent forecast at USD 72 a barrel for the fourth quarter.
